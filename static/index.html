How it works

Say a phone number, a PIN or an amount. With your explicit credit consent, SankhyaGuard sends your voice to two Gnani speech models, reads the numbers itself with its own Kannada, Hindi and English number parser, and compares them.

If they agree, it accepts. If they don't, it asks you, by voice, about the part in doubt, and you answer "one" or "two". A normal app would have accepted the first guess silently.
Prototype; model agreement is not proof of correctness and shared errors can pass unnoticed. Only complete saved clean-pilot results are available in Replay. Live audio is sent to Gnani for transcription only after consent and an explicit recording or demo-processing action. Audio is held temporarily for processing and is not written to the app's logs.
